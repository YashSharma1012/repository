Digital marketing FAQs

Straight answers to the questions Melbourne business owners ask us most.

How much does digital marketing cost in Melbourne?

Most Melbourne small businesses invest between $1,500 and $6,000 per month depending on services and goals. We provide a clear, fixed quote after a free audit.

How long does SEO take to work?

Most clients see early movement in 8 to 12 weeks, with strong results at 6 to 12 months. SEO compounds over time, so the longer you invest, the better it performs.

Do you require long-term contracts?

No. We work on flexible monthly agreements because we want to earn your business every month.

Do you work with businesses outside Melbourne?

Yes. We are Melbourne-based but work with clients across Victoria and Australia.

What platforms do you build websites on?

We build on WordPress and Shopify, and custom-code when a project needs it. Every site is mobile-first and optimised for speed and SEO.

How will I know my marketing is working?

You receive a monthly report covering traffic, leads, sales and return on ad spend, plus a regular strategy call.

What is included in the free marketing audit?

A review of your website, search rankings, ads and competitors, with a prioritised list of opportunities. No obligation.
